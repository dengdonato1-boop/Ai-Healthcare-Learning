## What I Learned

### Introduction to Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing healthcare by helping doctors, hospitals, and researchers improve medical services and patient care. AI systems can analyze large amounts of healthcare data faster than humans and help improve decision-making.

AI can support:

- Disease detection
- Medical imaging analysis
- Healthcare automation
- Personalized treatment
- Patient monitoring
- Drug discovery
- Medical research

AI does not replace healthcare professionals. Instead, it helps support doctors and medical staff in making better decisions.

## Understanding Healthcare Data Types

Healthcare systems use different types of data.

Examples include:

### Electronic Health Records (EHR)

These contain patient information such as:

- Medical history
- Diagnoses
- Treatment records
- Lab results
- Medications

### Medical Imaging Data

Examples include:

- X-rays
- MRI scans
- CT scans

AI can help analyze these images faster.

### Genetic Data

This helps healthcare professionals understand diseases linked to genetics.

### Wearable Device Data

Devices such as smart watches collect:

- Heart rate
- Sleep information
- Physical activity

## Privacy and Security in Healthcare AI

Healthcare information is sensitive and must be protected.

Important areas include:

### Data Privacy

Patient information must remain confidential.

### Data Security

Healthcare systems must protect information from cyberattacks.

### Access Control

Only authorized people should access medical records.

### Encryption

Sensitive healthcare information should be protected.

As a cybersecurity student, I understand that privacy and security are critical in healthcare systems.
